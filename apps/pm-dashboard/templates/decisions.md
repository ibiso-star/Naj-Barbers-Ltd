# Decisions — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

> New file — no prior charter section covered this. An ADR-style (Architecture
> Decision Record) log of significant technical/product decisions and *why*,
> including what was rejected and why. Distinct from `PRD.md` §11 (Risk
> Exceptions — an accepted risk, not a general decision) and §13 (Open
> Questions — still unresolved, not yet decided). Only log decisions worth
> someone asking "wait, why did we do it this way?" months later — not every
> small implementation choice.

## Format

Copy this block per decision, newest first.

```markdown
### {{DATE}} — {{ONE-LINE DECISION TITLE}}

**Status:** proposed / accepted / superseded by [{{later decision}}]

**Context:** {{what prompted this decision — the problem or constraint}}

**Decision:** {{what was decided}}

**Alternatives considered:** {{what else was on the table, and why each was rejected}}

**Consequences:** {{what this makes easier, harder, or forecloses later}}

**Decided by:** {{who}}
```
