### 8.9 Legal & compliance baseline
- [ ] 🔴 [LEGAL-001] Terms of Service present *(mark N/A only for genuinely internal-only/pre-launch prototypes with no public users — not as a default escape hatch)*
- [ ] 🔴 [LEGAL-002] Privacy Policy present *(same condition)*
- [ ] 🟡 [LEGAL-003] Data Processing Agreement drafted if handling other businesses' user data
- [ ] 🟡 [LEGAL-004] Refund Policy present if selling anything
- [ ] 🟡 [LEGAL-005] Master Service Agreement drafted if selling to other businesses/enterprise
- [ ] 🟡 [LEGAL-006] Data retention/deletion policy considered, not "we'll figure it out later"
- [ ] 🔴 [LEGAL-007] *(If operating under UK/EU law)* Cookie/tracking consent obtained **before** non-essential analytics load (PostHog and similar are non-essential under PECR) — a written policy alone doesn't satisfy this; the consent gate has to actually run before the script fires. Confirm the exact consent mechanism and severity with counsel — this is a judgment call on risk appetite, not a settled fact
- [ ] 🔴 [LEGAL-008] *(If operating under UK/EU law)* Operational ability to action a Data Subject Access Request or erasure request within the statutory window (one month under UK GDPR) — this is a working capability, not just the retention *policy* noted above
- [ ] 🔴 [LEGAL-009] **Termly (or equivalent) is a baseline only** — upgrade to real per-jurisdiction legal counsel if the project handles sensitive data, medical/safety information, minors, payments, or regulated activity
