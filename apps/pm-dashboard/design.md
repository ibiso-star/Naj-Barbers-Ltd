**Constraints**
- Brand voice: plain, terse and exact — like good engineering tooling. Sentence case, short labels, no exclamation marks, no marketing language inside the product — casual or professional
- Design direction: want: Linear's density, hairline borders, restrained colour and speed; keyboard shortcuts. Don't want: purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism, glowing orbs, heavy drop shadows, rounded-everything cards, or a pastel light theme — minimal editorial, playful 3D, glass UI, etc.
